Taking a good photo

  1. One item at a time: the whole thing in the picture, from the front.
  2. Lay it flat or hang it: on a bed, the floor or a hanger. The hanger is taken out.
  3. A plain background that stands out: dark clothes on a light sheet, light clothes on something dark.
  4. Even light: daylight near a window is best. Avoid harsh shadows and the flash.
  5. Other clothes in the shot? Tap "Pick one piece" and then the one to keep.
  6. Color or warmth wrong? Fix it under Edit in your closet.
